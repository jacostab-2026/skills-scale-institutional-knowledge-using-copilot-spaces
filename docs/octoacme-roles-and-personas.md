# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors or Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic sponsorship, organizational alignment, and escalation support for important initiatives. They help ensure that the work remains connected to business priorities and that major blockers receive timely attention.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm that project goals, scope, and success measures align with business strategy
- Approve major scope, funding, or priority changes that exceed the team's normal decision boundaries
- Support escalations when Project Managers, Product Managers, or engineering leads need leadership intervention
- Review key milestones, major risks, and outcomes with delivery and product leadership

### Goals
- Keep initiatives aligned to business outcomes
- Remove organizational blockers that could delay delivery
- Reinforce clear accountability for major decisions and trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Executive reviews, steering meetings, and milestone checkpoints
- Escalation discussions with Project Managers and Product Managers when timeline, budget, or scope risks increase
- Sponsor updates that summarize decisions, risks, and next steps

---

## Product Owners or Business Leads

### Role Summary
Product Owners represent the business outcome for a project, translating customer and operational needs into priorities the team can deliver. They work closely with Product Managers and Developers to keep the backlog, acceptance criteria, and release scope aligned to value.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities, acceptance expectations, and readiness criteria for planned work
- Make day-to-day scope and priority decisions within the agreed roadmap and project goals
- Clarify trade-offs with Product Managers, Project Managers, and Developers when requirements change
- Provide or approve key deliverables such as feature briefs, acceptance criteria, and business sign-off

### Goals
- Ensure the team is building the highest-value work first
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and business decisions
- Keep customer and operational outcomes visible throughout delivery

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement, sprint planning, and feature review sessions
- Ongoing coordination with Product Managers on roadmap intent and with Project Managers on sequencing and dependencies
- Escalation of unresolved business trade-offs to sponsors or governance checkpoints when priorities conflict

---

## Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads focus on day-to-day execution across teams, helping translate project plans into coordinated delivery. Where Project Managers manage overall planning and reporting, Delivery Leads stay close to implementation progress, dependencies, and execution risks.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate execution across Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers to keep work moving
- Track delivery milestones, blockers, and cross-team dependencies at the working level
- Drive follow-up on risks, action items, and decisions needed to maintain delivery flow
- Produce key deliverables such as delivery plans, dependency tracking, and execution status summaries

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery against agreed commitments
- Surface execution risks early enough for mitigation or escalation
- Improve coordination between planning and implementation teams

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups, dependency reviews, and milestone tracking sessions
- Close coordination with Project Managers on schedules and escalations, and with Developers on blockers and technical sequencing
- Escalation of delivery issues through existing status reporting and risk-management routines when commitments are at risk

---

## Risk or Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Risk or Compliance Leads help teams identify, assess, and manage delivery, regulatory, or policy risks that could affect project outcomes. They bring structure to risk governance and ensure that required controls, reviews, and mitigations are completed.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain visibility into material risks, controls, and compliance obligations tied to the project
- Advise Project Managers, Product Managers, and Developers on mitigation plans and review requirements
- Recommend when issues require escalation because they threaten commitments, policy compliance, or customer trust
- Produce or review key deliverables such as risk registers, mitigation plans, control evidence, and compliance checkpoints

### Goals
- Reduce delivery surprises caused by unmanaged risk
- Ensure project work meets policy, regulatory, and governance expectations
- Improve confidence that teams can make informed go/no-go decisions

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews, control checkpoints, and governance meetings
- Collaboration with Project Managers on risk tracking and with Delivery Leads and Developers on mitigation actions
- Escalation through project governance channels when risks exceed agreed tolerance or require sponsor decisions

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the readiness activities required to move completed work into production or customer-facing environments. They help teams align testing, approvals, communications, and timing so that releases are predictable and well-governed.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release scope, sequencing, and readiness criteria with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers
- Coordinate approvals, dependency checks, rollback planning, and release communications
- Make or recommend go/no-go decisions based on agreed readiness inputs and escalation outcomes
- Maintain key deliverables such as release plans, readiness checklists, cutover timelines, and post-release summaries

### Goals
- Deliver stable releases with clear ownership and preparation
- Reduce release-day surprises and coordination gaps
- Ensure deployment decisions are supported by the right stakeholders and evidence

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews, deployment windows, and post-release follow-ups
- Coordination with Developers on technical readiness and with Product Managers on business readiness and communications
- Escalation to Project Managers, sponsors, or governance reviews when release risks affect delivery commitments or customer impact

---

## Stakeholder or Customer Advocates

### Role Summary
Stakeholder or Customer Advocates represent the voice of affected users, customers, or partner groups during planning and delivery. They help the team validate that decisions remain practical, understandable, and aligned with real-world needs.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate stakeholder concerns, feedback, and adoption considerations
- Help Product Managers and Product Owners validate that priorities reflect customer and business needs
- Identify communication, training, or change-management deliverables needed for successful rollout
- Raise issues when stakeholder impact, usability, or expectations are not being addressed adequately

### Goals
- Keep customer and stakeholder outcomes visible during delivery
- Improve alignment between delivered work and user expectations
- Reduce adoption risks caused by missed communication or feedback loops

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings, feedback reviews, and launch-readiness discussions
- Collaboration with Product Managers, Project Managers, and Release Managers on scope, messaging, and rollout planning
- Escalation through normal governance and status channels when unresolved stakeholder concerns threaten project success
