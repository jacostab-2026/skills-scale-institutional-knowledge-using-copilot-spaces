# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project-management approach for cross-functional delivery work. Projects begin with a lightweight initiation step to confirm the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, and capture an initial timeline, risks, and resource needs before moving into planning.

Once work is approved, planning turns the initiative into a prioritized backlog of shippable increments with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, a release plan, a Definition of Done, and an initial QA approach. Execution and tracking then follow a regular team rhythm of standups, delivery syncs, and milestone demos, supported by a project board, small pull requests, CI checks, and metrics tied back to the original success measures.

Risk management and communication stay active throughout the lifecycle. Teams maintain a risk register, review dependencies, use a single source of truth for status, provide regular stakeholder updates, and escalate blockers through clear PM, Product Lead, and sponsor paths. Release and deployment focus on meeting acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, preparing release notes and rollback plans, validating staging and production deployments, and communicating the release clearly.

Retrospectives and continuous improvement close the loop after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents by identifying what went well, what should improve, and which action items should be assigned and tracked. Across the lifecycle, Project Managers coordinate delivery, Product Managers define outcomes and priorities, Developers build and test solutions, QA/Testing validates readiness, and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
